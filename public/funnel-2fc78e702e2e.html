How to read this

Counts are a floor, not a total: nothing is recorded unless a visitor accepts the cookie banner, so real traffic is higher by an unknown factor. Rates between steps stay valid, because both numbers come from the same consenting visitors — trust the percentages, not the absolutes. A visit that lands and leaves without navigating or interacting still counts as a session, because the landing page is recorded.
